About

He/Him
b. 2000

I work with sculpture, painting and installation. At the moment, I am interested in landscapes and the attitudes and arrangements adopted towards matter and its use purposes. In my process, the subject, the objects and the space are of equal value and in constant dialogue with each other.

I don’t prefer to disguise the process or the material, on the contrary I often tend to praise it. Simple forms, coincidences, two-dimensionality, lines and errors are essential and recurring elements in my work and works.

I graduated with a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree from the Turku Arts Academy in 2026. At the moment I live and work in Turku, Finland.
